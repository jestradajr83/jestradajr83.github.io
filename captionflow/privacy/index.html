CaptionFlow
Privacy Policy — a Reel Routine extension

Last updated: October 25, 2026

CaptionFlow is a browser extension published by Reel Routine. This policy describes exactly what the extension does with your information.

The short version

CaptionFlow collects nothing. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything it does happens locally in your browser, on your device.

What the extension processes

When you use CaptionFlow, you may paste script text or load caption files (SRT/VTT) from your device. That content is processed entirely in your browser to generate caption files for you to download. It is never transmitted to Reel Routine or to any third party, and it is not stored by the extension after you close the page (other than the file you choose to download).

What the extension stores

The extension requests Chrome's local storage permission for one purpose only: to remember an optional Pro license key on your own device, so you do not have to re-enter it each session. That key stays on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the extension's stored data in Chrome settings.

What the extension does NOT do

  • It does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, fingerprinting, or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not make network requests.
  • It does not read your browsing history, your tabs, or the contents of websites you visit.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of CaptionFlow ever changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the version history noted here before that version is released.
